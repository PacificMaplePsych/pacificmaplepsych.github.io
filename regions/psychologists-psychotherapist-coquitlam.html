From the Tri-Cities, getting downtown is easier than many people expect. The Millennium Line connects Lafarge Lake-Douglas, Coquitlam Central and Moody Centre to the Expo Line, and on weekdays the West Coast Express runs commuter trains from Port Coquitlam, Coquitlam Central and Moody Centre straight to Waterfront Station, steps from our office.

Many Tri-Cities families use a mix of both: virtual therapy during the week, and in-person visits for assessment testing at our office at 470 Granville Street.

How Tri-Cities Clients Use Our Services

Train to the door

Waterfront Station, the last stop on the West Coast Express, is a short walk from the Rogers Heritage Building. SkyTrain riders can transfer from the Millennium Line to the Expo Line at Lougheed Town Centre and ride to Granville Station.

Help for OCD and anxiety

Some of our clinicians use Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP), the leading evidence-based therapy for obsessive-compulsive disorder, along with CBT and ACT for anxiety, panic and avoidance.

Teens and young adults

Our virtual groups for adolescents (13 to 18) and young adults (19 to 29) run for eight weeks with no more than eight members, so teens can join from home after school.

Services for Coquitlam Clients

Therapy for Anxiety and OCD

For worry that will not switch off, panic attacks, intrusive thoughts and checking or reassurance-seeking. Sessions are available in person or by secure video, and may include structured exposure work when it fits your goals. Learn more about individual therapy.

Group Therapy

Virtual eight-week groups for adolescents, young adults and adults, plus a one-time drop-in supportive group for adults. Groups focus on social confidence, communication and practical coping skills. Learn more about group therapy.

Psychological Assessment

Psychoeducational and diagnostic assessments for children, youth and adults, covering learning, attention, memory and emotional functioning. Testing happens at our downtown office. Learn more about psychological assessment.

Autism Assessment

Comprehensive autism assessments for all ages, combining interviews, observation and standardized measures, with written recommendations for families, schools and workplaces. Learn more about autism assessment.

Questions from Coquitlam Clients

Is there an office in Coquitlam?

No. Our office is in downtown Vancouver, reachable by SkyTrain or the West Coast Express. Therapy can also be fully virtual.

Do you offer ERP for OCD?

Yes. Some of our clinicians use Exposure and Response Prevention. Mention OCD when you book so our team can match you with someone who offers it.

Can my teenager join a group?

Yes. The adolescent group is for ages 13 to 18, runs virtually for eight weeks, and is led by registered psychologists. A $200 deposit confirms registration and is applied to session fees.

How long does an assessment take?

It usually starts with a 1.5 to 2 hour virtual interview, followed by one to three in-person testing sessions of about four hours each, online questionnaires, and a one-hour feedback session.
