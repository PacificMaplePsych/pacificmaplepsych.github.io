Questions from Abbotsford Clients

Can I do all my therapy online from Abbotsford?

Yes. Individual and group therapy can be fully virtual from anywhere in BC.

Why does an assessment need an in-person visit?

Many standardized tests used for ADHD, learning and autism assessments need to be given face to face to be valid. The clinical interview and questionnaires are completed remotely beforehand.

Is there a Punjabi-speaking clinician?

Yes. Gurjot Chhina offers sessions in English and Punjabi under the supervision of a licensed psychologist.

What does it cost?

Fees depend on the clinician and service. Supervised student therapy is $120 per session and group therapy is $60 per session. Contact us for current rates for other services.
