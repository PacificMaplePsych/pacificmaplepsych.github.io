Richmond is one of the easiest places to reach us from. The Canada Line runs from Richmond-Brighouse, Lansdowne, Aberdeen and Bridgeport stations straight to Waterfront Station, which is steps from our office in the Rogers Heritage Building at 470 Granville Street.

For many Richmond clients, being able to speak in their first language matters. Dr. Howard Wong, a Licensed Psychologist, provides assessment, individual therapy and couples therapy in English, Cantonese and Mandarin.

What to Know as a Richmond Client

Care in Cantonese and Mandarin

Dr. Wong works with youth and adults on depression, anxiety, trauma, grief and substance use, and with couples. Ask for him by name when you book.

Adjusting to change

Moving countries, caring for aging parents, or raising children between two sets of expectations can be stressful. Therapy offers a confidential place to sort through it at your own pace.

Virtual when it suits you

If the commute does not fit your week, sessions can be held by secure, encrypted video from home or a private space at work.

Services for Richmond Clients

Individual Therapy

For anxiety, low mood, grief, loneliness and stress, including sessions in Cantonese or Mandarin with Dr. Wong. Available in person downtown or by video. Learn more about individual therapy.

Couples Therapy

For partners dealing with recurring conflict, emotional distance, parenting stress or changing roles. Dr. Wong also offers couples therapy in Cantonese and Mandarin. Learn more about couples therapy.

Psychological Assessment

Diagnostic and psychoeducational assessments for youth and adults, from ADHD and learning concerns to clarifying mood and anxiety symptoms, with a written report and feedback session. Learn more about psychological assessment.

Psychiatric Consultation

Our consulting psychiatrists see adults for psychiatric assessment and medication. MSP-covered psychiatric care requires a referral from your physician, and our psychologists can help coordinate one for current clients. Read about our consulting psychiatrists and our referral policy.

Questions from Richmond Clients

Is there a psychologist who speaks Cantonese or Mandarin?

Yes. Dr. Howard Wong offers assessment, individual therapy and couples therapy in English, Cantonese and Mandarin.

How do I get to your office from Richmond?

Take the Canada Line to Waterfront Station, the last stop. Our office is a short walk away at Unit 619, 470 Granville Street.

Can I see a psychiatrist through your clinic?

Our consulting psychiatrists see adults. A physician referral is needed for MSP-covered psychiatric care, and if you are already working with one of our psychologists, they can help coordinate it.

Do you work with couples?

Yes. Couples therapy is available for concerns such as conflict, distance, trust and parenting stress, including in Cantonese and Mandarin with Dr. Wong.
