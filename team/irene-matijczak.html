Feeling stuck? Overwhelmed? Wondering how to move forward? Life can become difficult for many reasons. Relationship challenges, workplace stress, anxiety, perfectionism, health concerns, trauma, grief, or major life transitions can leave you feeling overwhelmed, discouraged, or unsure of what comes next. You don't have to navigate it alone.

For more than 25 years, I have helped children, youth, adults, couples, and families create meaningful change and improve their quality of life. I work with people from all walks of life (including students, parents, leaders, entrepreneurs, athletes, and veterans) who are looking to strengthen their relationships, build resilience, improve communication, navigate difficult experiences, and move beyond patterns that have kept them feeling stuck.

My approach is holistic, heart-centred, collaborative, and forward-thinking. I integrate Counselling Psychology, Executive Coaching, Applied Neuroscience, Lifestyle Medicine, Performance Psychology, Sports Psychology, Life Coaching, and Child and Family Counselling to create practical, personalized strategies that support lasting growth. As a Registered Clinical Counsellor with a Master's degree in Counselling Psychology from Adler University and a Certified Clinical Trauma Professional, I combine evidence-informed approaches with genuine compassion and encouragement.

Whether you're struggling with anxiety, low self-esteem, stress and burnout, trauma, grief, relationship difficulties, separation and divorce, family conflict, workplace bullying, chronic pain, diabetes, or adjusting to surgery or other health challenges, my goal is to provide a safe, non-judgmental space where you feel heard, understood, and supported.

I believe meaningful change is possible. With the right support and practical tools, improving your quality of life doesn't always have to take as long as you might think. Together, we'll build on your strengths, explore new possibilities, develop psychological resilience, and create lasting progress toward a life that feels more fulfilling, purposeful, and aligned with the future you want.

I look forward to meeting you and supporting you on your journey forward.
