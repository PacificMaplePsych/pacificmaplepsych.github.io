Which group is right for you?

Groups are organized by age so you can connect with people at a similar stage of life. Read more about each group before you register.

Adolescents (13 to 18)

80-minute weekly sessions over eight weeks, with up to eight members.

Young adults (19 to 29)

80-minute weekly sessions over eight weeks, with up to eight members.

Adults (30 to 59)

80-minute weekly sessions over eight weeks, with up to eight members.

Supportive Group (19+)

A one-time, 50-minute drop-in session with up to ten members.

Good to know before you book

Led by registered psychologists

Every group is facilitated by a registered psychologist and draws on cognitive behavioural skills, mindfulness, and real-life practice.

Fully virtual

All groups meet by secure video, so you can join from anywhere in British Columbia.

Cost and registration

Group therapy is $60 per session. The eight-week program fee of $480 applies regardless of attendance, and a $200 deposit confirms your spot.

Small groups

Age-based groups have a maximum of eight members, and the Supportive Group has up to ten, so everyone has room to take part.

Combine with individual therapy

Many people pair a group with individual therapy for more time on their own history and goals.

Spaces are limited

Registering early is recommended. Questions before you sign up? Call 604-774-5501 or book a free consultation.
