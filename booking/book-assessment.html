Good to know before you book

No referral needed

You can request an assessment yourself, and you do not need to know exactly which assessment you need.

What happens after you submit

Our practice manager typically follows up within one business day to recommend the right assessment and clinician and explain the cost.

How the assessment works

Most assessments start with a virtual interview, followed by in-person testing at our downtown Vancouver office, a written report, and a feedback session.

Insurance and fees

Many extended health plans reimburse assessments by registered psychologists, and unreimbursed fees can be claimed as a medical expense. See our policies.

Reports for school, work, and funding

Depending on the findings, your report can support accommodations and applications for disability supports. See our disability resources.

International visitors welcome

We have assessed visitors from outside Canada. You need to be physically in Canada for every part of the assessment, including video appointments.
