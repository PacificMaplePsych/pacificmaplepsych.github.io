Good to know before you book

No referral needed

You can book therapy yourself. A physician referral is only needed for MSP-covered psychiatric care.

In person or online

Meet at our office in the Rogers Heritage Building, steps from Waterfront Station, or by secure video anywhere in BC.

Insurance and fees

Many extended health plans reimburse therapy with registered psychologists and clinical counsellors. Select clinicians are registered with CVAP. See our policies for details.

Faster access to psychiatry

Clients in ongoing therapy can be referred internally to our consulting psychiatrists for an expedited, MSP-covered consultation.

Care in your language

Sessions are available in English, Cantonese, Mandarin, Punjabi, and Urdu.

Cancellation policy

Please give at least 48 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Our policies page explains the fees.
