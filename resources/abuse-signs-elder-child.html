Signs of Elder
& Child Abuse

This page has been split into two pages so you can find what you need faster:

  • Signs of Child Abuse: emotional abuse, neglect, physical abuse, sexual abuse, grooming, and online sextortion
  • Signs of Elder Abuse: physical, emotional, and financial abuse, neglect, self-neglect, and scams

Important: in British Columbia, everyone has a legal duty to report if they believe a child or youth under 19 may be at risk. Call Provincial Centralized Screening at 1-800-663-9122, 24 hours a day. For concerns about an older adult, call the Seniors Abuse and Information Line at 1-866-437-1940. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.
