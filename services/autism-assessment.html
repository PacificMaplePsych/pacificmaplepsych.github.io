While the clinical term is Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), we recognize that many people view autism as a natural variation in how the brain works rather than something ‘disordered.’ Our approach is neurodiversity-affirming and strengths-based.

Autism is a complex neurodevelopmental condition that can involve differences in social interaction, communication, and patterns of behaviour and interests.

Presentation varies from person to person and can include a wide range of strengths, support needs, and levels of daily-life impact.

Clear identification and understanding of autism can support effective planning, accommodations, and intervention.

At Pacific Maple Psych Centre, we offer comprehensive autism assessments for children, adolescents, and adults in Vancouver and across British Columbia.

Process

To begin the assessment process, we start with an in-depth intake session. This allows us to gather detailed information about developmental history and current concerns.

We then administer a series of measures tailored to the individual’s age and needs. These may include evaluation of social communication, adaptive functioning, behaviour patterns, and cognitive functioning.

Our team includes experienced psychologists who specialize in neurodevelopmental assessment. We use a multidisciplinary approach that may include interviews, observations, and standardized measures to build a comprehensive understanding of each individual’s unique needs.

At Pacific Maple Psych Centre, we believe that an accurate diagnosis can be a meaningful first step toward improved quality of life and more effective support.

Our assessments extend beyond diagnosis and aim to provide practical insights and recommendations for personalized interventions and support strategies. You will receive clear feedback, written documentation, and recommendations that can guide support at school, at work, and at home.

We emphasize collaboration with individuals, families, and other professionals involved in care to support a plan that reflects strengths, needs, and goals.

Contents of the Final Report

Children

  • You will receive a comprehensive formal report with a clear diagnosis, if applicable.
  • Completion of any necessary disability-related documentation and forms.
  • Completion of MCFD (Ministry of Children and Family Development) forms for autism funding is included in the cost of the assessment.
  • CLBC (Community Living BC) form completed and signed to support access to adult services. Families can begin the process before age 16; however, it is often most helpful to support a smooth transition to adult services beginning at age 19. If eligible and approved, individuals may receive benefits such as income and housing support, medical coverage, and employment assistance through CLBC.

Adults

  • A comprehensive formal report summarizing findings and explaining results.
  • A formal diagnosis, if applicable.
  • Documentation for disability-related purposes, IEPs, or workplace accommodations.
  • Completion of forms or applications for testing accommodations (for example, professional or standardized examinations), if pertinent to the diagnosis.
  • Practical recommendations for therapeutic, educational, or workplace support.
  • Strategies to cope with challenges.
  • Option for a follow-up consultation to review progress or determine next steps.

What happens after you submit the intake form

Booking an assessment starts with a short online intake form. You do not need a referral, and you do not need to know exactly which assessment you need.

  2. We schedule your appointmentsMost assessments begin with a virtual clinical interview. In-person testing at our downtown Vancouver office is usually offered within one to seven weeks.
  3. You receive your report and feedbackYour psychologist reviews the results with you in a feedback session and provides a written report with practical recommendations.

Faster access to a psychiatrist, if you need one

Some autistic people also live with anxiety, depression, or ADHD, and medication can be part of the picture. Clients who complete an assessment with us remain eligible for an internal psychiatric referral for six months afterward.

If your clinician believes a medication review or a second diagnostic opinion would help, they can recommend an expedited consultation with one of our consulting psychiatrists and, with your consent, coordinate the referral. Psychiatric consultations are covered by MSP, and your ongoing care continues with the clinician who already knows you.

Available to active clients: people in ongoing therapy with us (usually every two weeks), people working with one of our doctoral students, and people who completed an assessment with us in the past six months. MSP-covered psychiatric care requires a physician referral. Read our referral policy.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Who are autism assessments for at Pacific Maple Psych Centre?

We provide comprehensive autism spectrum disorder (ASD) assessments for children, adolescents, and adults at our downtown Vancouver office, for clients from across British Columbia. The process is tailored to each person's age, presentation, and support needs.

What does an autism assessment involve?

Our autism assessments begin with an in-depth intake to gather developmental history and current concerns. Depending on the person, the assessment may include interviews, observation, and standardized measures of social communication, adaptive functioning, behaviour patterns, and cognitive functioning, such as the ADOS-2 and ADI-R.

Do you assess adults for autism, including late diagnosis?

Yes. We assess adults, including people who are exploring autism for the first time in midlife. Our psychologists pay close attention to presentations that are often missed, such as masking, and to the overlap between autism, ADHD, anxiety, and trauma.

Do you assess children and teens for autism?

Yes. We assess children and adolescents for autism and provide a comprehensive formal report with a clear diagnosis if applicable, along with practical recommendations for home and school.

Is your approach neurodiversity-affirming?

Yes. While the clinical term is autism spectrum disorder, many people see autism as a natural variation in how the brain works. Our assessments are strengths-based and focus on understanding each person's strengths, needs, and next steps, not only on a diagnosis.

Will I receive a formal autism assessment report?

Yes. You will receive a comprehensive formal report summarizing the findings, explaining the results, and stating a diagnosis if applicable, along with practical recommendations and a feedback session.

Do you complete MCFD autism funding forms?

Yes. For children, completion of Ministry of Children and Family Development (MCFD) forms for autism funding is included in the cost of the assessment. Our disability resources page links to BC autism funding information.

Can your autism assessment support CLBC applications?

Yes. Where appropriate, we complete and sign the Community Living BC (CLBC) form to support access to adult services.

Can an autism assessment help with school, workplace, or testing accommodations?

Yes. Depending on the findings, the report may support disability-related documentation, individual education plans (IEPs), workplace accommodations, and accommodations for professional or standardized examinations.

How long is the wait for an autism assessment?

Wait times for autism assessments currently range from about two weeks to two months. After you submit the intake form, our team typically follows up within one business day to confirm the details and schedule your first appointment.

Can international visitors book an autism assessment?

Yes. We have provided assessments for international visitors, and people travelling to Vancouver for an assessment are welcome. You need to be physically in Canada for every part of the assessment, including any appointments held by video.

What happens after the autism assessment is complete?

You receive a formal report and practical recommendations. Many clients continue with support from our team, such as individual therapy or autism family support, and clients who completed an assessment with us in the past six months can be referred internally to one of our consulting psychiatrists for an expedited, MSP-covered consultation.
