Group Therapy

Pacific Maple Psych Centre offers virtual group psychotherapy across British Columbia. Our group therapy is designed to provide a supportive, structured environment where individuals can build skills, strengthen connection, and work through challenges.

Book Group Therapy

In our small group therapy sessions, you will have the opportunity to express your thoughts and emotions while surrounded by others who may share similar experiences. Our groups are designed to help guide and support you as you work toward personal and professional goals.

Regardless of your age or the topic you wish to explore, our group therapy sessions provide a safe and confidential space to express yourself, receive support, and develop practical coping strategies.

Approach

Our group therapy sessions focus on topics such as managing stress, anxiety, depression, panic disorder, and substance use, among others. Other areas of focus may be more general, such as improving social skills and working through personal challenges like anger or low self-esteem. Some of these skills may be practised through role-playing real-life social scenarios.

We incorporate evidence-based techniques such as cognitive-behavioural skills, mindfulness, and real-life exposure to provide practical tools that extend beyond the therapy setting.

Why people choose group therapy

Many people arrive believing they are the only one who feels the way they do. Hearing others describe the same worries is often where change begins. A group offers guidance and support, a fresh perspective, and a safe place to practise social situations that might otherwise feel overwhelming.

What a group can help you build

  • Confidence in social situations
  • Clearer, calmer communication
  • Practical coping skills for stress, anxiety, and low mood
  • A sense of connection and feeling less alone
  • Real-world practice through role-play and gradual exposure

Group, individual, or both?

  • Group therapy is a lower-cost way to learn skills alongside others
  • Individual therapy offers more time on your own history and goals
  • Many people combine the two
  • Groups are virtual, so you can join from anywhere in BC
  • Not sure? Our team can help you decide

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is group therapy at Pacific Maple Psych Centre?

Group therapy at Pacific Maple Psych Centre is structured, virtual psychotherapy for people across British Columbia. Small groups led by registered psychologists help participants build skills, receive support, and work through challenges alongside others with similar experiences.

Who is group therapy for?

Our groups are organized by age: adolescents aged 13 to 18, young adults aged 19 to 29, and adults aged 30 to 59. A one-time Supportive Group is also available for anyone aged 19 and older.

What topics are addressed in group therapy?

Topics may include stress, anxiety, depression, panic disorder, substance use, social skills, anger, low self-esteem, and other personal challenges. Sessions draw on cognitive behavioural skills, mindfulness, and role-playing real-life social situations.

Are group therapy sessions virtual?

Yes. All group therapy sessions are held by secure video, so you can join from anywhere in British Columbia.

How long are group therapy sessions, and how big are the groups?

Age-based groups meet for 80 minutes per session over eight weekly sessions, with a maximum of eight members. The Supportive Group is a single 50-minute session with up to ten members.

How much does group therapy cost?

Group therapy is $60 per session. The eight-week program fee of $480 applies regardless of attendance, and a $200 deposit, applied toward your session fees, is required to confirm registration.

Can I attend group therapy while seeing an individual therapist?

Yes. Many people combine group and individual therapy. Group sessions offer practice and connection, while individual sessions allow more time for your own history and goals.

What if group therapy is not the right fit for me?

Some people prefer one-to-one support. We also offer individual therapy for adolescents and adults in Vancouver and across British Columbia, including lower-cost sessions with supervised doctoral students.

Do you also offer psychological and autism assessments?

Yes. If you are looking for diagnostic clarity, learn more about our psychological assessments, including ADHD and psychoeducational assessments, and our autism assessments for children, teens, and adults.

How do I register for group therapy?

You can register through our group therapy registration form. Spaces are limited, so early registration is recommended.
