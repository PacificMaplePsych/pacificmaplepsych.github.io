At Pacific Maple Psych Centre, we understand that close relationships can be deeply meaningful and deeply challenging. Even the strongest partnerships go through periods of conflict, distance, or misunderstanding. When communication breaks down, trust feels shaken, or disconnection starts to grow, couples therapy can provide a structured space to slow things down and work toward real change.

Our couples therapy services support partners who want to improve communication, reduce recurring conflict, rebuild emotional closeness, and better understand the patterns affecting their relationship. We work with couples facing concerns such as conflict, emotional distance, infidelity, parenting stress, and major life transitions.

Whether you are feeling stuck in the same arguments, struggling to reconnect, or trying to decide what healthier next steps look like, we are here to help you and your partner move toward a more secure, respectful, and connected relationship.

How couples therapy works

Early sessions focus on understanding your relationship: what brought you in, the patterns that keep repeating, and what each of you hopes will change. From there, your therapist helps you slow down difficult conversations, recognize the cycle you get caught in, and practise new ways of responding to each other. Many couples also have some individual sessions along the way.

Our couples therapists draw on research-based methods, including the Gottman Method, emotion-focused therapy (EFT), and the Satir model. We welcome couples of all backgrounds and relationship structures, including consensually non-monogamous relationships, and sessions are available in person in downtown Vancouver or virtually anywhere in BC.

When couples therapy is not the right first step

If there is abuse, coercive control, or one partner feels afraid of the other, joint sessions can increase the risk of harm. In those situations, we recommend individual therapy first. It can help you make sense of what is happening, decide whether and when to leave, and plan for safety.

Our abuse resources explain the signs of domestic abuse and how to plan for safety. If you are in immediate danger, call 911.

Common Reasons Couples Seek Therapy

  • Communication problems, recurring arguments
  • Emotional distance or difficulty reconnecting
  • Trust concerns, including after infidelity
  • Parenting stress and family pressures
  • Major life transitions or changing roles
  • Wanting a healthier, more supportive relationship

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is couples therapy at Pacific Maple Psych Centre?

Couples therapy at Pacific Maple Psych Centre helps partners understand the patterns affecting their relationship, communicate more effectively, work through conflict, and rebuild connection and trust. Sessions are available in downtown Vancouver or virtually anywhere in British Columbia.

What issues can couples therapy help with?

Couples therapy can help with communication difficulties, recurring arguments, emotional distance, infidelity and trust concerns, parenting stress, neurodivergence in a relationship, and major life transitions such as moving, having children, or retiring.

When should a couple consider couples therapy?

You do not need to wait until things feel extreme. Therapy is often most effective when partners notice they are stuck in the same arguments, feel disconnected or misunderstood, or want support through a major change together.

What approaches do your couples therapists use?

Our couples therapists draw on the Gottman Method, emotion-focused therapy (EFT), the Satir model, and cognitive behavioural strategies. Your therapist will explain their approach in the first session and tailor it to your relationship.

Can couples therapy help with communication problems?

Yes. One of the main goals of couples therapy is healthier communication, so that partners can understand each other, express needs clearly, and respond with more empathy and less defensiveness.

Can couples therapy help after infidelity?

Couples therapy can help partners work through the impact of infidelity, understand what happened in the relationship, and decide whether and how trust can be rebuilt.

Is couples therapy appropriate if there is abuse in the relationship?

Usually not as a first step. When there is abuse, coercive control, or fear of a partner, joint sessions can increase risk. We recommend individual therapy instead, and our abuse resources include information on safety planning. If you are in immediate danger, call 911.

Do you work with LGBTQ+ and non-monogamous couples?

Yes. We welcome couples of all identities and relationship structures. Dr. Cosette LeBlanc has particular experience working with gender-diverse individuals and people in consensually non-monogamous relationships.

Do you offer couples therapy in Cantonese or Mandarin?

Yes. Dr. Howard Wong provides couples therapy in English, Cantonese, and Mandarin, drawing on the Gottman Method and the Satir model.

Can we do couples therapy online?

Yes. Couples therapy is available virtually anywhere in British Columbia, which also works well when partners join from different locations within the province.

What if one or both partners also need individual support?

Some couples benefit from one-to-one support alongside relationship work. You can learn more about our individual therapy services, and if a deeper evaluation would help, our psychological assessments.

How do we book couples therapy?

You can book online, call 604-774-5501, or email info@pacificmaplepsych.com. Let us know you are booking couples therapy so we can match you with a therapist trained in couples work.
