Therapy and Assessments

Therapy helps you improve how you feel, think, and relate to others. An assessment answers a specific question, such as whether autism, ADHD, a learning disability, or a mental disorder is part of the picture. Many of our clients do both, and moving between them is simple when your clinicians work on the same team.

Psychologists and psychiatrists, working on the same team

Psychologists and psychiatrists look at mental health from different angles. A psychologist takes the time to understand how you think, feel, and cope, and helps you change those patterns through assessment and therapy. A psychiatrist is a medical doctor who can establish or review a diagnosis and consider whether medication might help.

Many people need both, yet through the usual route a psychiatric consultation in BC can involve a long wait. At Pacific Maple, the two work side by side. If your clinician believes a medication review or a second diagnostic opinion would help, they can recommend an expedited consultation with one of our consulting psychiatrists.

Your care stays in one place. After the consultation, the psychiatrist sends a report to your family doctor or primary care provider, and you keep seeing the clinician who already knows your story.

Does Pacific Maple Psych Centre offer therapy, assessments, or both?

Both. We offer individual therapy, couples therapy, and group therapy, as well as autism assessments, ADHD, psychoeducational, mental health, and integrated psychological assessments, and independent medical evaluations. Many clients use both, for example by starting therapy after an assessment clarifies what is going on.

Do I need a referral to book?

No. You can book psychology and counselling services yourself, without a referral from a doctor. A physician referral is only needed for MSP-covered psychiatric consultations, which our team helps coordinate for active clients.

Is therapy covered by MSP or extended health insurance?

Psychology and counselling services are private-pay and are not covered by MSP, but many extended health plans reimburse services from registered psychologists and clinical counsellors. Please check your plan before your first session. Fees that are not reimbursed can be claimed as a medical expense on your taxes. Select clinicians are registered with the Crime Victim Assistance Program (CVAP), and B.C. Emergency Health Services (BCEHS) employees and their families can access support at no cost through our partnership with BCEHS. See our policies for details.

Are there lower-cost therapy options?

Yes. Supervised doctoral students offer therapy at $120 per session, and most insurers cover their sessions when the student is regularly supervised by a registered psychologist. Group therapy is $60 per session. For a limited time, doctoral student Sydney Harrison is also offering lower-cost therapy.

Can I see a psychiatrist through Pacific Maple Psych Centre?

Yes, as an active client. If you are in ongoing therapy with us, working with one of our doctoral students, or completed an assessment with us in the past six months, your clinician can recommend an expedited internal referral to one of our consulting psychiatrists for a consultation on diagnosis or medication. Psychiatric consultations are covered by MSP, the psychiatrist sends a report to your primary care provider, and your follow-up care continues with your psychologist or therapist.

Do you offer virtual appointments?

Yes. Individual and couples therapy are available in person in downtown Vancouver or virtually anywhere in British Columbia, and group therapy is fully virtual. Assessments usually combine a virtual interview with in-person testing at our downtown Vancouver office.

Do you work with children and teens?

Yes. We provide autism, ADHD, and psychoeducational assessments for children, teens, and adults. Individual therapy is available for teens and adults, several of our clinicians also work with children and families, and our group therapy program includes a group for teens aged 13 to 18.

Can international visitors book an assessment?

Yes. We have provided assessments for international visitors, and people travelling to Vancouver for an assessment are welcome. You need to be physically in Canada for every part of the assessment, including any appointments held by video.

What languages do your clinicians speak?

Care is available in English, Cantonese, Mandarin, Punjabi, and Urdu, and one clinician also speaks conversational Hindi. Let our team know your preferred language when you book.

How soon can I start?

Our therapists currently have openings for new clients, so therapy can often begin soon after you book. Assessments are typically scheduled within one to seven weeks, depending on the type of assessment and the clinician. You can book therapy online, complete the assessment intake form, or call 604-774-5501.
