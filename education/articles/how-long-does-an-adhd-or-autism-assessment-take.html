How Long Does an ADHD or Autism Assessment Take

This page reflects our understanding of mental health topics as of 2026. If you are reading this at a later date, some details may no longer be current, so please verify anything important against up to date sources. If you notice anything that needs updating, we would welcome you letting us know.

An ADHD or autism assessment usually involves several appointments spread over a few weeks, with a total of several hours of direct time, followed by a few weeks for scoring and report writing, and then a feedback session. From the first appointment to receiving results, the whole process often takes about one to two months, though this varies by clinic, the complexity of the questions, and whether ADHD and autism are assessed together. Wait times before the assessment begins are separate and can range from short for some private clinics to many months or longer for public services. Asking the clinic for an estimated timeline at the start can help you plan.

How the Timeline Breaks Down

  1. Intake. Completing forms and questionnaires, sometimes before the first appointment.
  2. Clinical interview. Often one to two appointments of one to two hours each, covering history and current concerns.
  3. Collateral input. Questionnaires or interviews with a parent, partner, or friend, which can add time if they take a while to return.
  4. Testing or observation. Standardized measures of attention, executive functioning, or social communication, which may take a few hours in one or more sessions. The pages about ADHD assessment appointments and autism assessment appointments describe what happens.
  5. Scoring and report writing. Usually a few weeks after the final appointment.
  6. Feedback session. About an hour to review findings and recommendations, as described on the page about feedback sessions.

Practical Details

Single versus combined assessments. Assessing both autism and ADHD usually takes longer than assessing one, as explained on the page about being assessed for both.

Complexity. Co-occurring conditions, such as anxiety, depression, trauma, or learning differences, may require additional measures.

Your schedule. How quickly appointments can be booked and how promptly forms are returned both affect the timeline.

Wait times. Private clinics may have shorter waits than public services, though this varies. The page about private versus public assessment compares options.

Breaks and energy. Testing can be tiring, so some clinics spread sessions out or include breaks, which may lengthen the schedule but improve accuracy.

Urgent needs. If you need results by a specific date, such as for school accommodations, mention this when booking.

How to Plan

  • Ask for an estimated timeline. Including when the report and feedback are likely.
  • Complete forms promptly. Delays in paperwork can delay the assessment.
  • Ask collateral contacts early. Let family or a partner know they may be asked to help.
  • Block time for appointments. Plan for rest afterward, especially on testing days.
  • Plan follow-up. If medication may be considered after an ADHD diagnosis, book a doctor's appointment after you expect your report.
  • Prepare in advance. See the page about preparing for an ADHD or autism assessment.

Common Questions About ADHD and Autism Assessment Timelines

How long does an ADHD or autism assessment take from start to finish?

From the first appointment to results, an ADHD or autism assessment often takes about one to two months, though this varies by clinic and complexity.

Is an ADHD evaluation usually finished in a single visit?

Not usually. An ADHD evaluation typically needs several appointments, including interviews, possible testing, and a feedback session.

How many weeks after testing does the autism report arrive?

An autism assessment report usually takes a few weeks after the final appointment, because scoring and writing take time.

Does adding autism to an ADHD evaluation extend the timeline?

Yes. Assessing both ADHD and autism usually takes longer than assessing one, since more areas need to be explored.

Why do some ADHD or autism assessments take longer than others?

Co-occurring conditions, collateral input, scheduling, and whether both ADHD and autism are assessed can all lengthen an assessment.

How long are the wait times for ADHD or autism assessments in BC?

Wait times for ADHD or autism assessments in BC vary widely, with private clinics often shorter than public services.

Can I speed up my ADHD or autism assessment?

Completing forms promptly, asking family for input early, and booking appointments quickly can help an ADHD or autism assessment move faster.

How long is each testing session in an autism assessment?

Autism assessment testing or observation sessions may last a few hours, sometimes split across more than one appointment.

What if I need ADHD assessment results by a certain date?

Mention any deadline when booking your ADHD assessment, such as for school accommodations, so the clinic can advise on timing.

When should I book a doctor's appointment after an ADHD assessment?

Book a doctor's appointment for after you expect your ADHD assessment report, especially if you want to discuss medication.
