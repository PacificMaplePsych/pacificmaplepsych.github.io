How Long Does Couples Therapy Usually Take

This page reflects our understanding of mental health topics as of 2026. If you are reading this at a later date, some details may no longer be current, so please verify anything important against up to date sources. If you notice anything that needs updating, we would welcome you letting us know.

Couples therapy often lasts somewhere between about 8 and 20 sessions, though some couples need only a few and others continue for a year or more. Length depends on what you are working on, such as communication, recurring conflict, or recovering from an affair, how long the difficulties have been present, the therapy approach, how often you attend, and how engaged both partners are. Emotionally focused therapy, for example, is often described as a short-term approach of around 8 to 20 sessions. Recovery from a major betrayal or long-standing distress typically takes longer. Your therapist can give you a rough estimate after the first few sessions and review progress along the way.

How It Works

Assessment phase. The first few sessions usually involve understanding your history as a couple, each partner's perspective, and your goals. Many therapists include an individual session with each partner, as explained on the page about whether both partners need to attend every session.

Active work. The middle phase focuses on changing patterns, such as how you handle conflict, express needs, and reconnect. Approaches such as the Gottman Method and emotionally focused therapy each have their own structure.

Consolidation and ending. As things improve, sessions may become less frequent. The final sessions focus on maintaining progress and planning for future challenges.

Practical Details

Factors that shorten therapy. Starting early, before patterns become entrenched, clear shared goals, consistent attendance, and practising between sessions.

Factors that lengthen therapy. Long-standing conflict, betrayal, one partner being unsure about staying, individual mental health concerns, or trauma affecting the relationship. The page about rebuilding trust after a betrayal discusses why this process takes time.

Frequency. Weekly or every-other-week sessions are common at the start. Spacing sessions too far apart early on can slow progress.

Session length. Couples sessions are often 50 to 90 minutes. Longer sessions can allow more depth for some couples.

Cost. Fees and coverage vary. Some extended health plans cover couples therapy with eligible providers, so check your plan.

Discernment counselling. When one partner is unsure whether to stay, a short focused process may come before or instead of full couples therapy.

How to Make the Most of It

  • Agree on goals. Clear, shared goals help therapy stay focused.
  • Attend consistently. Regular sessions build momentum.
  • Practise between sessions. Try the skills and conversations you work on.
  • Check in on progress. Ask your therapist every few sessions how things are going and whether the plan needs adjusting.
  • Be patient with setbacks. Conflict may feel worse briefly before it improves, as difficult topics are opened up.
  • Address individual concerns. Individual therapy for one partner may run alongside couples work when needed.

If there is abuse or fear in the relationship, joint couples therapy may not be safe. Resources are available on the abuse resources page.

Common Questions About How Long Couples Therapy Takes

How many sessions of couples therapy do most couples need?

Many couples attend somewhere between about 8 and 20 sessions, though some need fewer and others continue for a year or more.

Why does couples therapy take longer for some couples?

Couples therapy may take longer when there is long-standing conflict, betrayal, uncertainty about staying, or individual mental health concerns.

How long does couples therapy take after an affair?

Couples therapy after an affair often takes longer than for other concerns, commonly many months, because rebuilding trust is a gradual process.

Is emotionally focused therapy for couples short-term?

Emotionally focused therapy for couples is often described as short-term, around 8 to 20 sessions, though length varies with each relationship.

How often should we attend couples therapy at the start?

Weekly or every-other-week couples sessions are common at the start. Spacing them too far apart early on can slow progress.

How long is each couples therapy session?

Couples therapy sessions are often 50 to 90 minutes, and longer sessions can allow more depth for some couples.

Can couples therapy get worse before it gets better?

Yes. Conflict between partners may feel worse briefly as difficult topics are opened up, before improvement follows.

What is discernment counselling for couples?

Discernment counselling is a short process for couples where one partner is unsure about staying, helping them decide whether to pursue couples therapy.

What signs show a couple is ready to end counselling?

A couple is usually ready to finish couples therapy when goals are met, you can handle conflict on your own, and you and the therapist agree to taper and end.

Can we return to couples therapy after we finish?

Yes. Many couples return for a few sessions during stressful times or major life changes, even after successfully finishing couples therapy.
