CBT for Health Anxiety

Cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) for health anxiety helps people who worry intensely about having or developing a serious illness to understand the cycle that keeps the worry going and to change it. That cycle usually involves noticing a body sensation, interpreting it as a sign of danger, and then checking, searching online, or seeking reassurance, which brings brief relief but makes the fear return stronger. CBT helps people examine catastrophic interpretations, gradually reduce checking and reassurance-seeking, and face health-related fears in manageable steps. It has strong research support, and many people notice a meaningful drop in health worry within a few months.

How CBT for Health Anxiety Works

Health anxiety is not simply worrying about health. It is a pattern where ordinary body sensations, such as a headache, a skipped heartbeat, or a twinge, are read as signs of serious disease, and the worry takes over daily life. Anxiety itself produces physical sensations, as described on the page about physical symptoms of anxiety, which can add to the fear.

CBT for health anxiety focuses on several parts of the cycle:

  • Interpretations. Learning to notice catastrophic thoughts, such as "this headache means a tumour," and weigh them against more likely explanations.
  • Attention. Understanding how focusing on the body makes sensations more noticeable and more alarming.
  • Safety behaviours. Gradually reducing checking the body, searching symptoms online, repeated doctor visits for reassurance, and avoiding health-related reminders.
  • Exposure. Facing feared situations in steps, such as reading about an illness, sitting with uncertainty, or postponing a check.
  • Tolerating uncertainty. Accepting that no test can guarantee perfect health, and learning to live well anyway.

These ideas build on general cognitive behavioural therapy principles, adapted for health fears.

What Sessions Are Like

Early sessions explore your specific health worries, what triggers them, and what you do when they arise. Together, you and the therapist map out your personal cycle. Many people are surprised to see how reassurance-seeking and checking keep the worry alive.

Later sessions involve experiments and practice. You might test what happens to a sensation when you pay close attention to it compared with when you shift focus, or practise delaying a symptom search and noticing how anxiety rises and falls on its own. Between sessions, you track worries and gradually cut back on safety behaviours. CBT for health anxiety often involves around eight to sixteen sessions and can be delivered in person or online.

A good therapist will also encourage appropriate medical care. The goal is not to ignore your health, but to have a clear, agreed plan with your doctor, such as regular check-ups, rather than frequent visits driven by anxiety. If a new symptom is severe or sudden, such as chest pain or trouble breathing, seek medical care or call 911.

Who It Can Help

CBT can help people who spend a lot of time worrying about illness, checking their bodies, searching symptoms, or seeking reassurance from doctors, family, or the internet. It can also help when worry centres on a family member's health. It is useful when health anxiety occurs alongside panic, generalized anxiety, or OCD-like checking. The difference between health anxiety and related conditions is explained on the page about health anxiety versus somatic symptom disorder.

Exposure is a central part of this work, similar to the approach described on the page about how exposure therapy treats phobias. When health anxiety comes with significant depression or a recent traumatic medical experience, those may need attention too. Medication prescribed by a physician is sometimes used alongside CBT.

Common Questions About CBT for Health Anxiety

How does CBT treat health anxiety?

CBT for health anxiety helps you notice catastrophic interpretations of body sensations, reduce checking and reassurance-seeking, and face health fears in steps.

How does CBT help me cut back on searching symptoms online?

CBT for health anxiety helps you notice the urge, delay or skip the search, and see that anxiety eases on its own, which weakens the cycle over time.

Will CBT for health anxiety tell me to stop seeing my doctor?

No. CBT for health anxiety encourages appropriate medical care with an agreed plan, rather than frequent visits driven by worry.

How many sessions of CBT does health anxiety usually need?

CBT for health anxiety often involves around eight to sixteen sessions, depending on how long the worry has been present.

Which habits keep health anxiety going even though they feel protective?

Safety behaviours in health anxiety include body checking, symptom searching, repeated reassurance-seeking, and avoiding health reminders.

Why does watching a body symptom closely make my health anxiety spike?

Paying close attention to a symptom makes it more noticeable and alarming, which is a key part of the health anxiety cycle CBT works on.

What does exposure look like in CBT for health anxiety?

Exposure for health anxiety might mean reading about an illness, delaying a body check, or sitting with uncertainty while anxiety rises and falls.

Can CBT help if my health anxiety is about my child or partner?

Yes. CBT can address health anxiety focused on a loved one's health, using the same steps of reducing checking and reassurance.

Can I do CBT for health anxiety online?

Yes. CBT for health anxiety can be delivered by video or through guided online programs as well as in person.

Is medication ever used alongside CBT for health anxiety?

Sometimes. A physician may prescribe medication alongside CBT for health anxiety, especially when it occurs with depression or other anxiety.
